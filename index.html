Joey Clothing / a redesign in trust

UX case study · 2026

A teardown of a live Sri Lankan e-commerce brand. Five usability and trust failures found across the customer journey, then the mobile flow rebuilt from registration to checkout, with a strategy report and a published Figma prototype.

  • UX research
  • Mobile redesign
  • E-commerce
  • Figma

02

30 Years of Vijayism / a career in one room

Conceptual portrait · 2022

Two portraits of the actor, gold-framed legacy above and present-day everyman seated below, surrounded by thirty years of artefacts. Awards on the floor, the debut-film magazine on the wall.

  • Conceptual
  • Composite
  • Editorial

03

Seylune / luxury fashion frontend

Frontend website · 2026

A fictional luxury fashion store built for a web development assignment: a dark purple and gold visual system and a complete frontend across home, category, product, cart, account, search and about pages.

  • HTML / CSS / JS

04

Avengers: Doomsday / two versions, one lesson

Cinematic composition · 2025

Started with twenty-seven characters and a single-tone palette, then threw it out. Rebuilt with a controlled cast, a dual-Doom hierarchy and a green, purple and warm palette that finally read as Doomsday.

06 About

I taught myself design in 2019 with free YouTube tutorials and a copy of Photoshop.

I'm Thinura Anjana, Marketing Executive at Jeewa Education and the designer behind FORGE Studio. I started designing the year I finished my O/Ls and took on my first client work in 2020.

Seven years on, my work has aired on Indian television, placed first out of two hundred entries, and grown into FORGE Studio: a practice where cinematic design meets campaigns and digital product thinking.

Alongside work, I'm reading for a BBA (Hons) specialising in Management Information Systems at SLIIT, and I design event campaigns as Co-Editor of the SLIIT Business School Student Community.
