02 Cinematic Composition 2025

Avengers: Doomsday — two versions, one lesson.

A self-directed cinematic poster study. Started with twenty-seven characters and a single-tone palette. Threw it out. Rebuilt with a controlled cast, dual-Doom hierarchy, and a green-purple-warm palette that finally read as a Doomsday. Process documented across five spreads.

05 — About

I started teaching myself design six years ago. A lot has changed since.

I’m Thinura Anjana — a designer and second-year MIS student at SLIIT University, Sri Lanka. I started designing in 2019, the year I finished my Ordinary Levels, with nothing but free YouTube tutorials and a copy of Photoshop.

Six years on, my work has reached Indian television, won a national course-level competition, and grown into an independent studio practice — FORGE — that sits at the intersection of cinematic design and digital product thinking.

Currently building. Available for internships from June 2026.
