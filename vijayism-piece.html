1,300°C Case study
Case 02 / Conceptual tribute / 2022

30 Years of
Vijayism.

Aired on Thanthi TVPhoto-compositeScratch-built scene

A celebration poster built as a room of memory: a framed legacy above, a grounded present below, and career artefacts hidden across the wall, floor, furniture and light.

DesignedEnd of 2022
Purpose30-year celebration
RoleConcept, compositing
BuiltLayer by layer
Broadcast 2022Thanthi TV anniversary coverage
01 The idea

A career compressed into one cinematic room.

The poster is not just a portrait. It is a room full of evidence. The larger framed figure creates legacy and scale, while the seated figure makes the tribute feel close, grounded and human.

Around them, objects work like clues: films, awards, magazine memories, fan moments and small symbols from different points of the journey.

  • Legacy
  • Memory

  • Room design
30

2022

Indian television recognition, on Thanthi TV.

02 Built from scratch

Wall, floor, light and props, shaped by hand.

The final image reads as one room, but it was designed piece by piece: Photoshop layers, textures, lighting passes, masking, shadows, colour grading and depth.

Layer 01

Architecture first

Wall, wooden furniture, floor pattern, frame, shelves and room boundaries came first, so the poster had a believable physical space.

Layer 02

Objects as storytelling

Awards, magazine, autograph, books, newspaper, phone and gloves guide the viewer through the thirty-year celebration.

Layer 03

Lighting to unify

Warm gold from the frame and lamp balanced against deep green shadows, for a vintage, celebratory and cinematic mood.

03 Reference direction

Inspired by a first look. Rebuilt as a tribute.

The spark came from the first-look poster of the Tamil film Japan, released in 2022 ahead of the film in 2023. The influence is the idea of a character-centred staged room, not a copy of the image.

In

Inspired by

A staged-room composition with a strong central figure and cinematic object placement.

Out

Reinterpreted as

A career tribute where every prop supports the celebration, not just the aesthetic.

How

Designed from scratch

Wall, floor, frame, props, shadows, highlights and colour mood, manually built through layered compositing.

04 Hidden details

Find the five hidden details.

The objects are small scenes inside the scene. Tap the glowing points on the poster to uncover each one and the story behind it.

05 Recognition

From social tribute to television frame.

The clip shows the artwork appearing on Thanthi TV in the actor's anniversary coverage, proof that the composition travelled well beyond a personal design experiment.

Medium

Cinematic tribute poster

Role

Concept, compositing, room build, effects

06 What it taught me

Hide details, but never hide the main idea.

Balance

Structure before detail

The viewer must first read the emotional structure: legacy above, presence below, memory around them.

Composition

A frame first

Without a clear frame, strong hierarchy and a believable room, hidden details become clutter.

Craft

Craft builds credibility

Building the wall, floor, light and shadows by hand made it one controlled scene, not pasted elements.
